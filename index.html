The syllabus

Every repair follows the same four lessons.

DiagnoseRobin reads the error and the script together and names the root cause, not just the line that crashed.
RepairYou get the complete fixed script back, ready to drop into your resource or place.
ExplainA short note on why it broke, so the same mistake doesn’t catch you twice.
ProtectRobin won’t break licensing, escrow or encryption, and won’t write cheats or exploits.
